# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Ryan (solopreneur product/UX designer) and his AI agents, who build Craftwell apps on top of Quill — the system exists so agentic builds come out on-brand without re-deciding design each time.
Secondary: visitors to quilldesignsystem.com — developers, designers, and potential clients evaluating the system (and, by extension, Ryan's craft) before installing it or working with him.

## Product Purpose

Quill is a complete design system — tokens, four themes, components, blocks, docs — distributed as a shadcn registry and consumed by Ryan's own apps (SkillDecks and others). Success is twofold: (1) every app built on Quill ships fast and looks finished with near-zero per-app design effort, and (2) the system itself is a public showcase of that success that others can see, browse, and install.

## Positioning

One designer's taste, turned into a system that people and AI agents can ship: warm, printed, accessible by default.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Browse the docs (Storybook at /storybook/).
- Secondary CTA: install it — `npx shadcn add https://www.quilldesignsystem.com/r/quill.json`.
- The line a visitor remembers: "A design system, made for people."
- Belief ladder: this looks genuinely crafted → it's a real system (tokens, themes, foundations), not a skin → the components are comprehensive, tested, and accessible → I can have it in my project with one command.
- Proof on hand: the site itself is built from Quill components; a live Storybook with tested, WCAG-checked components; 49 installable blocks; four switchable themes; version-stamped releases.

## Brand Personality

Warm, unhurried, quietly confident — "a letterpress shop that sells apps" (DESIGN.md). The brand feels printed, collected, and kept: a naturalist's sketchbook of pressed cream paper, sepia ink, and a small cast of botanical pigments used sparingly.

## Anti-references

- The generic SaaS/shadcn default look: white cards, blue accents, hero-metric rows, identical card grids.
- Cold gray enterprise-dashboard flavor — the opposite of the letterpress voice.
- Loud startup maximalism: neon gradients, glassmorphism, purple-glow dark mode.
- Standing visual bans from DESIGN.md: no pure white, no pure black (Classic themes excepted), no emoji, no decorative gradients.

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: every Quill surface (homepage, docs) is built from Quill's own components and tokens.
- Made for people: accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) is a feature of the brand, not a checkbox.
- Agent-ready: tokens, usage docs, and llms.txt are single-sourced so AI agents build on-brand without guessing.
- Paper first: everything sits on digital paper — warmth comes from the material, not decoration.
- The system removes decisions: shipping fast is the product; per-app design choices are the enemy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA conformance target across components and site surfaces. Reduced-motion alternatives for animation. CVD-safe chart color system (fixed-order categorical, sequential, and diverging ramps).
