# ai-notice (component)

One quiet line under an AI composer saying the AI can be wrong, with an optional link the app supplies.

### When to use
- A composer sends to an AI and people should know its answers need checking.

### Reach for instead
- **alert** — when something has actually gone wrong and needs the person to act.
- **ai-badge** — when one piece of content was made by AI and needs labelling where it sits.

### Rules
- **Do:** Keep the notice to one small centred line under the composer, always present. **Don't:** Turn it into a banner, a callout box or a toast — it is there every time, so it stays quiet.
- **Do:** Leave it in muted ink, which passes 4.5:1 in every theme. **Don't:** Lighten it further with opacity — a disclaimer people cannot read does not inform them.

### Accessibility
- Plain paragraph text; the optional link is a normal underlined link with its own words.

### Design tokens
`--muted-foreground`
