# suggested-prompts (component)

Ready-made things to ask an AI — chips for follow-ups under an answer, cards for starters on an empty page, a list in a narrow panel. Picking one fills the composer; it never sends.

### When to use
- An AI page is empty and people need a first thing to ask.
- An answer has natural next questions worth offering.

### Reach for instead
- **command** — when people pick from a long, searchable list of actions.
- **button** — when the choice runs an action straight away instead of filling the composer.

### Rules
- **Do:** Fill the composer and focus it, so people can edit before sending. **Don't:** Send the suggestion the moment it is clicked — people lose the chance to adjust it.
- **Do:** Use chips under an answer, cards on an empty AI page, and the list in a narrow panel. **Don't:** Put large starter cards under every answer — they crowd the thread.

### Accessibility
- A labelled list of buttons; each button's text is the suggestion, so it reads well out of context.

### Design tokens
`--border` · `--background` · `--card` · `--input` · `--muted` · `--ink-soft` · `--foreground` · `--muted-foreground`
